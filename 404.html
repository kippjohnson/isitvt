Clinical safety

An undifferentiated wide-complex tachycardia should be presumed VT until proven otherwise. Do not delay resuscitation or cardioversion to run an ECG algorithm.

Regular + monomorphic?

The algorithms collected here were developed primarily for regular monomorphic WCT. Irregular or polymorphic rhythms require a different pathway.

12-lead · RBBB/LBBB branches

    04 · Compare

    Choose the tool that fits the tracing.

    No algorithm is a gold standard. Use a familiar method consistently, then reconcile it with clinical probability, the full ECG and—when needed—EP confirmation.

    Pooled validation

    High sensitivity. Moderate specificity. Meaningful heterogeneity.

    A 2023 meta-analysis of 14 studies and 3,966 patients found substantial between-study heterogeneity. That is a strong reason to show the source, population and limitations—not just a seductive yes/no output.

    Scope

    Education, not a treatment directive.

    IS IT VT? is intended for licensed clinicians and supervised medical trainees. It is an educational synthesis of published ECG methods, not a medical device, validated clinical calculator, substitute for local protocols, or replacement for expert consultation.

    Algorithms can misclassify rhythms. Management must integrate hemodynamic status, the complete clinical context, serial/baseline ECGs, electrolytes, medications, device data and definitive electrophysiologic evidence when available.
